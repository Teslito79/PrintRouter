Paper tests for walk-in orders (branch walk-in-rebuild, 2026-11-17)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Get Quote: a small order (an A4 B&W document, 2 pages x 2) for a test customer. Place it, put a
   PDF in its folder, Print Jobs, Print now. The cover sheet comes out first: plain A4, black and white,
   one side, with the name, phone, the number large, the total and the items. Then the document.
2. A colour product (e.g. 100 business cards, a laid-out A4 PDF, confirm 13 sheets): it prints at
   actual size on the routed copier from the Bypass Tray, on the cover sheet's copier after it.
3. Both sides on heavy paper (Silk 300, Both sides): fronts, then Print backs on the copier's block,
   backs. Does each back land on its own front, the right way up?
4. Counter, Today: the test prints show under Walk-in with #<number> (the cover sheet as one A4
   B&W side). Then Orders: Ready for pickup, Collected; the folder is in Walk-in\Customers.
5. Walk-in layout: Get Quote with 50 A5 flyers, Place order. The flyer folder has W<n>-1.cdr and
   Options.txt. Drop the customer's design in (it shows as a customer file, 'Waiting for layout'),
   lay it out in W<n>-1.cdr, export W<n>-1.pdf into the same folder, Orders > Layout done (25
   sheets suggested), then Print Jobs > Print now: only W<n>-1.pdf prints, at actual size.
6. Queue: three test customers, two copiers. A: two B&W orders; B: a B&W order of 30 sheets; C: a B&W
   order of 2 sheets; and a colour order for A. Print Jobs > Start printing orders: the IM C4510 prints
   A's two orders (each with its cover sheet), the IM C3000 A's colour order. Drag C's line above B's in
   the IM C4510's list (a blue line shows where it lands). Pages collected on the IM C4510: C prints next.
   Close and reopen ORCA: the order of the list is the same.
7. Drop: Get Quote, a document item, Place order. Drag a PDF from Explorer (or a USB stick) onto
   'Drop the file to print here' in the window that shows the number: it is copied into the item's
   folder (the original stays) and the order shows Ready to print. Drag a second PDF onto it: ORCA
   asks before replacing.
   Then Add Email: drag a PDF from Explorer into the box next to Add item (Document chosen): it is
   listed there and Pages shows its page count. Add item, Place order: the file is in the item's folder.
8. One ORCA: double-click ORCA twice; only one opens (the second click brings it to the front).
   Minimise it and double-click again: it comes back.
9. Customers and loyalty (needs prices in Walk-in_Prices.xlsx first): Walk-in > Customers, note a
   customer's "12 months" amount. Place a small order for them (Get Quote, their phone: no question),
   print it, Ready for pickup, Collected. Customers again: their 12 months and all time went up by the
   amount paid, and the order is listed under them.
10. Don't keep: Get Quote with a phone ORCA doesn't know: Place order asks "First order from this
   customer...". Press Don't keep. The cover sheet shows name and phone. Collected: the order's folder
   is gone from Walk-in\Open, it is not in Customers (nor in the Recycle Bin), and
   Walk-in\sales_log.csv has its line with no name.
