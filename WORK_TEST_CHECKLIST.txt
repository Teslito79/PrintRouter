Paper tests for walk-in orders (branch walk-in-rebuild, 2026-11-17)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Get Quote: a small order (an A4 B&W document, 2 pages x 2) for a test customer. Place it, put a
   PDF in its folder, Print Jobs, Print now. The cover sheet comes out first: plain A4, black and white,
   one side, with the name, phone, the number large and the items, and no prices anywhere on it
   (Tesla, 2026-11-17). Then the document.
2. A colour product (e.g. 100 business cards, a laid-out A4 PDF, confirm 13 sheets): it prints at
   actual size on the routed copier from the Bypass Tray, on the cover sheet's copier after it.
3. Both sides on heavy paper (Silk 300, Both sides): fronts, then Print backs on the copier's block,
   backs. Does each back land on its own front, the right way up?
4. Counter, Today: the test prints show under Walk-in with #<number> (the cover sheet as one A4
   B&W side). Then Orders: Ready for pickup, Collected; the folder is in Walk-in\Customers.
5. Walk-in layout: Get Quote with 50 A5 flyers, Place order. The flyer folder has W<n>-1.cdr and
   Options.txt. Drop the customer's design in (it shows as a customer file, 'Waiting for layout'),
   lay it out in W<n>-1.cdr, export W<n>-1.pdf into the same folder, Orders > Layout done (25
   sheets suggested), then Print Jobs > Print now: only W<n>-1.pdf prints, at actual size.
6. Queue: three test customers, two copiers. A: two B&W orders; B: a B&W order of 30 sheets; C: a B&W
   order of 2 sheets; and a colour order for A. Print Jobs > Start printing orders: the IM C4510 prints
   A's two orders (each with its cover sheet), the IM C3000 A's colour order. Drag C's line above B's in
   the IM C4510's list (a blue line shows where it lands). Pages collected on the IM C4510: C prints next.
   Close and reopen ORCA: the order of the list is the same.
7. Drop: Get Quote, a document item, Place order. Drag a PDF from Explorer (or a USB stick) onto
   'Drop the file to print here' in the window that shows the number: it is copied into the item's
   folder (the original stays) and the order shows Ready to print. Drag a second PDF onto it: ORCA
   asks before replacing.
   Then Add Email: drag a PDF from Explorer into the box next to Add item (Document chosen): it is
   listed there and Pages shows its page count. Add item, Place order: the file is in the item's folder.
8. One ORCA: double-click ORCA twice; only one opens (the second click brings it to the front).
   Minimise it and double-click again: it comes back.
9. Customers and loyalty (needs prices in Walk-in_Prices.xlsx first): Walk-in > Customers, note a
   customer's "12 months" amount. Place a small order for them (Get Quote, their phone: no question),
   print it, Ready for pickup, Collected. Customers again: their 12 months and all time went up by the
   amount paid, and the order is listed under them.
10. Don't keep: Get Quote with a phone ORCA doesn't know: Place order asks "First order from this
   customer...". Press Don't keep. The cover sheet shows name and phone. Collected: the order's folder
   is gone from Walk-in\Open, it is not in Customers (nor in the Recycle Bin), and
   Walk-in\sales_log.csv has its line with no name.
11. Prices by hand (needs prices in Walk-in_Prices.xlsx): Get Quote, 250 business cards 85 x 55, Silk 300,
   both sides. Work it out on paper: 32 A4 sheets (8 per A4), 64 colour copies, 8 cuts (4 x 2) per stack,
   so 64 x your A4 colour copy price + 32 x the Silk 300 A4 sheet price + 8 x the price per cut. Then a
   document: 10 pages, both sides, 2 copies, B&W: 20 copies + 10 sheets of Normal 80. ORCA's working
   lines under each item and its prices must match your paper sums.
12. Copy counter (it now reads the copier's counter page and splits the copies by itself):
   Walk-in > Copy counter, Start counting on the IM C4510. The panel must say "The copier counts B&W /
   colour, A4 / A3 and the sides by itself. The paper is given at Finish.", with no paper, copy type or sides buttons (if
   it shows the yellow "Counting from the total counter: pick the copy type", the page wasn't read: note
   that). Press nothing and make 2 A4 B&W copies, 1 A3 colour copy, then 2 A4 B&W copies both sides.
   Within a few seconds the panel shows A4 B&W 2 copies on 2 sheets (one side) + 4 copies on 2 sheets
   (both sides), so "A4 B&W: 6 copies on 4 sheets (2 one side, 2 both sides)", and "A3 colour: 1 copy on
   1 sheet (one side)". While it counts, a walk-in order can still print on the IM C4510 and doesn't
   change the count. Finish, then Print receipt: it prints on the XP-80C with the date, the copier and
   the lines, no shop name and (for now) no prices. Done.
   Papers at Finish: Start counting again, copy 10 sheets on 100 gsm and 10 sheets on 200 gsm. At
   Finish pick "Different papers": set the first row to the 100 gsm paper, add the 200 gsm paper with
   10 sheets (the first row shows 10 by itself, "20 of 20 A4 sheets given"). Print receipt: it lists
   both papers. Done.
   Then on the MP C3004: Start counting (it says "pick the size and the sides"), make 1 B&W
   and 1 colour A4 copy: it shows 1 A4 B&W and 1 A4 colour by itself. Cancel.
13. Settings: open Settings from the menu with the Prices password. It shows the three copiers
   (IM C4510, IM C3000, MP C3004) with their Windows printers, none in red, and Which printer does
   what as today: Black and white IM C4510, Colour documents MP C3004, Everything else IM C3000 (if
   busy IM C4510). Press Check address on each copier and note what it says. Change nothing, Back.
14. Many files and the Orders list: Add Email, Document, drop 5 PDFs at once on the box next to
   Add item. The box says "5 files: Add item makes 5 document items with these settings". Add item:
   there are 5 items, each named by its file with its own pages. Then main menu > Orders: each order
   is one line (number, customer, status, date) with Details...; Details... shows the items and files.
15. Inventory with the shop's barcode scanner: Inventory management > Add item, give it a name, a
   sale price (needs the Prices password) and 5 in stock, then scan the item's barcode into the
   Barcodes box. OK. On the Inventory screen scan it again: it gets selected. Press Sell, scan it once:
   it is in the basket with its price. Print receipt, then Done. Back in Inventory the stock is 4.
16. Rotate sort (its first paper test): Add Email, Document, A4, Normal 80 gsm, 1 copy, tick Rotate
   sort, drop 4 one-page A4 PDFs at once, Add item, place the order and print it. The 4 sheets come out
   of the IM C4510 crosswise one after another (Tray 1, Tray 4, Tray 1, Tray 4). Then one PDF with 3
   copies and Rotate sort: 3 sheets, crosswise.
17. When the IM C3000's new yellow toner goes in: within 10 minutes (or press Read now on the Data
   screen) Data records the change by itself (Yellow, automatic) and takes one spare yellow off. If it
   doesn't, press Toner replaced for it.
18. Out of toner: while the IM C3000 has no yellow (Data shows "Empty" and "Out of yellow toner:
   colour jobs go to IM C4510"), add a colour flyer or a colour document on Silk paper in Add Email.
   Print Jobs shows it going to the IM C4510 with "IM C3000 is out of yellow toner: sent to IM C4510".
