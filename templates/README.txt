CorelDRAW files that PrintHub copies into every product item.

blank.cdr         an empty CorelDRAW document (A4). Used for every product that
                  has no file of its own below.

<product>.cdr     optional: your layout template for one product, named after the
                  website's product id, e.g. bcard.cdr (8 business cards per A4 with
                  cut marks). Used instead of blank.cdr for that product.

The copy is named after the order: PH-10240.cdr (or PH-10240-01.cdr, PH-10240-02.cdr
when one order has several products). It is made once, when the order arrives, and
never replaced. In CorelDRAW, export the finished sheet as a PDF into the same
folder: CorelDRAW suggests PH-10240.pdf, keep that name. Then press Layout done in
PrintHub.
